📄 Dissertation

Fan Engagement in Football

My final year dissertation project examined how football clubs leverage digital platforms, community initiatives, and content to build genuine fan engagement. I explored case studies from across the Scottish Football Pyramid and the wider European football landscape, analysing what separates clubs that truly connect with fans from those that don't. My key findings where that successful fan engagement is built on authenticity, two-way communication, and a deep understanding of the fanbase. I also identified that the biggest factor in fan enagement is the ownership model of the club finding that Fan Owned clubs tend to have stronger fan engagement.
