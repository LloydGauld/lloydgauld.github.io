Hi! I'm Lloyd, a Business Management with Marketing graduate from Edinburgh Napier University, with a deep passion for sports marketing — especially football. I'm driven by the intersection of fan culture, digital content, and commercial strategy.

My dissertation explored fan engagement in football, diving into how clubs and brands build communities and loyalty in a digital-first world. I'm now channelling that research into written content and video analysis on financial issues in football.

Outside of football, I coach Rocket League competitively and have a love for travel — both of which have sharpened my communication skills, strategic thinking, and cultural awareness.

I'm currently looking for opportunities in sports marketing, fan engagement, content, and commercial roles — ideally in football but open to wider sports and esports.
